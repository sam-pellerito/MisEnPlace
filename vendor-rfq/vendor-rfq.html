What's in the template

The Excel file has two sections. The top is read-only context for the vendor — your company name, SKU, volume requirements, constraints, and operational notes. The bottom is their response section with clear labels for every field they need to fill in.

── YOUR REQUIREMENTS (read-only) ──────────────────
Company · SKU · Annual volume · COGS budget · Max lead time · MOQ · Certs · Incoterm
 
── VENDOR RESPONSE — fill in highlighted cells ──────
Vendor name · Contact · FOB unit price · MOQ · Lead time · Lead time range
Max monthly capacity · Payment terms · Country of origin · Port of export
Freight estimate · Certifications held · Sample availability · Notes

Vendor fills in the yellow cells and returns the file to you. You then upload it in Step 3.

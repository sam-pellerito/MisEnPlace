Primary SKU — demand & supply

Safety stock is extra inventory held to protect against demand spikes or supplier delays. More variability, longer lead times, and higher service levels all require more safety stock.

Average units sold per day in your "normal" month. Seasonality multipliers scale this up or down each month.
Day-to-day variation in sales. Use 15–25% of average demand if unsure.

Seasonality — monthly demand multipliers

Enter a multiplier for each calendar month. 1.0 = base demand. 1.3 = 30% above base. 0.7 = 30% below base. Green inputs are above average; red are below.

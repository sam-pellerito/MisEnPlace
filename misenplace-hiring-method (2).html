MisEnPlace · Hiring Method

Set the station
before you hire.

A kitchen doesn't trust the menu — it trusts mise en place: everything checked, prepped, and in its place before service starts. This runs the same discipline on hiring. Build the scorecard once. Then walk every past role with the identical five questions. The story that stays consistent across every station is the one you can trust.

The role, defined before you meet anyone

Write the scorecard first, candidate-blind. This is what "done well" looks like in the job — not a list of traits, a list of outcomes.

Check what matters for this seat. These become the lens you read station answers through — not extra questions to ask.

One station per past job

Chronological, oldest first or most recent first — pick one and stay consistent across every candidate. Ask the same five questions at every station. The consistency is the method: it's much harder to keep a story straight five times running than once.

Reading the ticket

Ratings compare what the candidate says about themselves against what they say others would say. A gap isn't disqualifying — but an unexplained one is worth a direct follow-up question.
